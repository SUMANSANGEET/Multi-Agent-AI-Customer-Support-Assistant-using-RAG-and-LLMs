# TechMart Global Refund & Return Policy (30-Day Window)

## Overview
TechMart offers a 30-day money-back guarantee for all hardware purchases, including ApexBook Pro 15, SoundBuds Ultra, SmartWatch Elite, and SmartHub Max.

## Conditions for Full Refund
1. Item must be returned in original packaging within 30 days of delivery.
2. Proof of purchase (order ID or invoice number) is required.
3. Digital Pro subscriptions ($14.99/mo) can be canceled anytime with pro-rated refunds for unused days within 14 days of purchase.

## Process & SLA
1. Initiate return request via Customer Portal or Chat Assistant.
2. Prepaid return shipping label will be generated instantly.
3. Refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after inspection.
